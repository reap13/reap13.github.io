Rea Pillai is currently a Quantitative Psychology PhD student at the University of Virginia. She earned her B.A. in Psychology with a minor in Data Science from the University of Virginia in 2026. Her research interests include network modeling, predictive modeling of psychological constructs, and intensive longitudinal data, with an emphasis on refining models to better capture mental healthcare disparities within vulnerable populations.
